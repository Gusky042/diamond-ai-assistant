# Privacy Policy – Nails by Diamond Assistant

**Last updated:** 26 November 2026

This policy explains how the **Nails by Diamond WhatsApp Assistant** ("Diamond", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information. Nails by Diamond is a nail business at ESUT Agbani, Enugu, Nigeria.

## What Information We Collect

When you chat with Diamond on WhatsApp, we may collect:

- **Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name**, so we can reply to you
- **The messages you send**, so we can understand and answer your questions
- **Booking details** you give us: your name, the service you want, and the date and time

We do **not** collect payment card details, passwords, or any information you do not send to us.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

- Answer your questions about our services and prices
- Make, confirm and manage your appointments
- Contact you about your booking

We do **not** sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Services That Help Us

- **WhatsApp (Meta Platforms)** delivers messages between you and Diamond.
- **Google Gemini (Google)** is an AI service that helps Diamond understand your messages and write replies. Your messages are sent to Google for this purpose only.

These companies process data under their own privacy policies.

## How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep booking records for as long as needed to provide our services, and for no longer than 12 months after your last appointment. Chat messages are not stored permanently.

## How We Protect Your Information

Your information is stored securely and is only accessible to the business owner. We take reasonable steps to protect it from loss, misuse or unauthorised access.

## Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

- Ask what information we hold about you
- Ask us to correct wrong information
- Ask us to delete your information
- Withdraw your consent at any time

## How to Delete Your Data

To delete your data, do either of these:

1. Send **"DELETE MY DATA"** to Diamond on WhatsApp, or
2. Email us at **nailsbydiamond1@outlook.com**

We will delete your information within 7 days and confirm when it is done.

## Children

Our service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us:
